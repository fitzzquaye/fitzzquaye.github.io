THE KINTSUGI CONNECTION

From fracture to possibility.

Kintsugi is the philosophical inspiration behind this journey: a reminder that fractures, imperfections and previous experiences do not need to be hidden or treated as the end of a story.

With care and intention, repair and transformation can create renewed value and a distinct history. Fitzgerald’s contemporary interpretation connects this deeper idea to his personal motto, entrepreneurial mindset, projects and the action-oriented work of StatNovation.

The philosophy travels through the portfolio: past experiences become material for learning; present projects are places to recognise potential and contribute thoughtfully; future ambitions remain possibilities for useful technology, enterprise, opportunity and meaningful progress.

Kintsugi is a Japanese philosophical and craft reference. Ghanaian and wider African heritage and historical craftsmanship are distinct traditions. This website connects them respectfully through a contemporary interpretation of wisdom, making, repair, continuity and transformation—not through any claim of shared origin.

StatNovation — project frame

StatNovation is one way Fitzgerald puts his kintsugi-inspired working philosophy into action: connecting technology, entrepreneurship, creativity and purposeful problem-solving. It is an umbrella direction in development, not a claim of completed outcomes.

Venue Grid — project frame

A venue discovery and booking platform concept. The potential recognised is a clearer Discover → Compare → Book → Pay journey. Fitzgerald’s contribution is concept development; verified deliverables and evidence can be added as they become available.

REMADE — project frame

A circular-design concept focused on the practical potential of materials treated as waste. Fitzgerald’s contribution is Canva and visual concept work. Status: participation.

BOWLBack — project frame

A reusable circular-container journey for restaurant customers. The problem considered is unnecessary single-use waste; the potential recognised is a reusable system connecting restaurants and customers. Status: concept.

eSchooling — project frame

Education-technology application work, including backend code and application features worked on. Further description and verified evidence should be added only when it accurately reflects the contribution.

AI Automation Programme — project frame

A proposed intensive online AI automation learning programme for Level 100 UPSA students. Its scope and delivery details remain to be confirmed.
